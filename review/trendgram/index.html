TrendGram: questions people ask

Is TrendGram legit?

TrendGram is an operating business with a public website (trendgram.io) and a public Trustpilot profile. Trustpilot has suspended its rating for breaching its guidelines: rating suspended, 213 reviews still visible, 57% one-star (30 Oct 2026). Its homepage shows “Rated 4.81/5”. Its Terms say that if monthly follower targets are missed it may use “genuine-appearing, and organically simulated automated accounts”, while the site says “No bots. No fake users.”

Why is TrendGram’s Trustpilot rating unavailable?

Trustpilot suspended it for showing Trustpilot content in a misleading way. Its profile shows: “This company’s rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines.” and “This company is displaying Trustpilot content in a misleading way.”

What do TrendGram’s bad reviews say?
