Is SocialFollow legit?

SocialFollow is an operating business with a public website (socialfollow.co) and a public Trustpilot profile. It is poorly rated there: 1.4 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 59 reviews, 83% of them one-star (30 Oct 2026). Its own site sells followers or engagement as packages. Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow buying or selling engagement such as likes, views and follows.
