Price and refunds

UpGrow: $69 to $129 a month. Path Social: prices not recorded here; see its own site.

UpGrow: A full monthly refund if there is no net-positive growth after the first 7 days, per its Terms (23 Sept 2026).

Path Social: 7-day money-back on a new purchase per its Terms; renewals not refundable (3 Sept 2026).

Which should you choose?

On the public record, trustpilot has suspended both ratings.

UpGrow sells followers or engagement as packages. Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow buying or selling engagement such as likes, views and follows.

If neither record suits you, three services are rated higher than both on Trustpilot, led by Virallized at 4.8 out of 5 from 92 reviews.

Whichever you pick, judge it by your own Instagram insights rather than the service’s dashboard.
