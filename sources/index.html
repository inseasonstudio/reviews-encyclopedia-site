Instagram, Meta and the FTC

View account and content insights on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram's free insights show an account's performance for any period within the last 90 days, so follower growth can be checked on the account itself rather than taken from a sales page.

Recommendations on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram lists accounts that have repeatedly used misleading practices to build a following, such as buying likes, among those it may not recommend to people who do not follow them. Repeatedly engaged in misleading practices to build followings, such as purchasing ‘likes’.

Spam (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta's spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows. Attempting to or successfully selling, buying or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows, clicks and use of specific hashtags.

Inauthentic Behaviour (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta removes fake accounts involved in inauthentic behaviour, which is why follower counts built on fake accounts fall when a clean-out reaches them.

Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials (14 August 2024)US Federal Trade Commission

The rule bans selling or buying fake indicators of social media influence, such as followers or views generated by a bot or hijacked account, where the buyer knew or should have known they were fake and used them to misrepresent their influence for a commercial purpose. It also bars a business from presenting a review site it controls as independent.

The Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule: Questions and AnswersUS Federal Trade Commission

The FTC's guidance says the rule took effect on 21 October 2024, and that followers who chose to follow an account are real people, not fake indicators.

Devumi, Owner and CEO Settle FTC Charges They Sold Fake Indicators of Social Media Influence (21 October 2019)US Federal Trade Commission

The FTC settled charges against Devumi, which sold fake followers, subscribers, views and likes to people who wanted to look more influential.

Check your Account Status on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Account Status, in Instagram's settings, shows whether anything posted on an account goes against Instagram's rules and may lead to restrictions, such as losing features or the account being taken down.

How to remove spam or bot followers on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram filters followers that are likely to be spam into a separate Potential spam list, which can be removed in one go without the accounts being told.

Instagram Ranking ExplainedInstagram (About Instagram)

Instagram says that in Reels, much like Explore, the majority of what people see comes from accounts they don't follow, so Reels are where an account is shown to new people.

Trustpilot Consumer Warnings and AlertsTrustpilot Help Center

Trustpilot places a Consumer Warning on a business's profile when it misuses the platform, for example by fabricating reviews, and keeps it until the misuse stops and a reasonable time has passed.
