About Reviews Encyclopedia

By Jay MercerUpdated 30 October 2026

Who runs it

Reviews Encyclopedia is owned and run by Virallized, an Instagram growth service founded in 2017 and one of the services rated here. Case files are written and checked by Jay Mercer, its founder.

We say this at the top of every page because it matters: a company that sells in a market is not a neutral judge of it. So nothing here rests on our opinion. The order is Trustpilot’s, the verdict is one fixed rule, and every figure is dated and linked so you can check it yourself.

Why it exists

Instagram growth is a market full of fake reviews. Trustpilot has withheld the ratings of 7 growth services for breaching its guidelines, most after removing fake reviews, and has removed 25 more growth sites’ profiles entirely.

Buyers asking which service to trust often find paid lists and review sites that never say who is behind them. This site is the opposite: the public record, read by hand and dated, by a company that says who it is.
