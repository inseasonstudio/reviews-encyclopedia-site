Trustpilot warnings · 29 October 2026

Instagram growth services Trustpilot has suspended or removed

By Sara Mihajlovic, analystCounts read from each public profile on the dates shown

Short version

7 ratings suspended, 25 profiles removed. Trustpilot has withheld the ratings of UpGrow, Path Social, Plixi, TrendGram, Crowd Ignite, GoreAd (Goread.io) and ProflUp for breaching its guidelines, and has removed 25 more growth sites’ profiles entirely.

Ratings suspended after fake reviews were removed

Each of these profiles is still live with its reviews visible, but shows no score, only Trustpilot’s words: “This company’s rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines.” and “We’ve removed a number of fake reviews for this company.”

Brands that have closed

Still searched for, no longer sold. Read between 23 September 2026 and 30 October 2026.

  • Jarvee: Its homepage reads “Service Permanently Closed”.
  • Instagress: Shut down in 2017; the domain shows a placeholder page.
  • Mass Planner: Closed; the domain is now an unrelated blog.
  • Keesy: The domain shows a parked-domain page with no product.
  • Growthoid: growthoid.com now redirects to SidesMedia, which lists no Instagram service; Trustpilot has removed both profiles.
  • Stormlikes: stormlikes.net now calls itself an editorial research site and says “We don’t sell these services”; Trustpilot removed its profile.

Sources

How to remove spam or bot followers on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram filters followers that are likely to be spam into a separate Potential spam list, which can be removed in one go without the accounts being told.

Trustpilot Consumer Warnings and AlertsTrustpilot Help Center

Trustpilot places a Consumer Warning on a business's profile when it misuses the platform, for example by fabricating reviews, and keeps it until the misuse stops and a reasonable time has passed.

Questions people ask

Why does Trustpilot suspend a rating?

For a breach of its guidelines, most often after it finds and removes fake reviews. The profile then reads “This company’s rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines.” with a notice such as “We’ve removed a number of fake reviews for this company.” or “This company is displaying Trustpilot content in a misleading way.”

Which Instagram growth services has Trustpilot suspended?

UpGrow, Path Social, Plixi, TrendGram, Crowd Ignite, GoreAd (Goread.io) and ProflUp. All were still withheld when read on 29 and 30 October 2026.

Which growth sites has Trustpilot removed?

Blastup, Bulkoid, Buzzoid, Famoid, GetAFollower, Goread.io, Growthoid, HighSocial, iDigic, Likes.io, MediaMister, Naizop, Poprey, RiseKarma, Rushmax, SidesMedia, SimplyGram, Socialplug, SocialWick, Stormlikes, Trollishly, Twicsy, UseViral, Views4You and Viralyft. Each was still removed when checked between 18 and 30 October 2026.
