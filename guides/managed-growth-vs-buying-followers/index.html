What each one sells

  • Managed growth: a team grows the account for you on a monthly plan.
  • Follower packages: a set number of followers, likes or views for a price. Meta’s spam policy does not allow buying or selling engagement.

What Instagram and Meta say

Spam (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta's spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows. Attempting to or successfully selling, buying or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows, clicks and use of specific hashtags.

Inauthentic Behaviour (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta removes fake accounts involved in inauthentic behaviour, which is why follower counts built on fake accounts fall when a clean-out reaches them.

How to remove spam or bot followers on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram filters followers that are likely to be spam into a separate Potential spam list, which can be removed in one go without the accounts being told.

Is managed Instagram growth better than buying followers?

On the Trustpilot record, managed services fare better: 4 of 18 are top or well rated, against 2 of 16 follower sellers, and the median one-star share is 31% against 50%. Buying followers is also against Meta’s spam policy.

Is buying Instagram followers against the rules?

Yes. Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows.

What does a managed growth service do?

A team grows the account for you on a monthly plan, rather than selling a number of followers.
