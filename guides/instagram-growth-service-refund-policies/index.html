Instagram growth service refund policies, compared

By Sara Mihajlovic, analystUpdated 30 October 2026

The short answer

Of the 32 services here with refund terms on record, 10 promise a money-back guarantee on the first payment, 6 refund only if the growth does not come, 13 refund case by case or only for orders not delivered, and 3 offer no refunds or publish no terms. Several promise more on the pricing page than their terms allow.

A money-back guarantee on the first payment

The service’s own site promises your money back within a set period, without having to show it failed. Several add conditions in their terms; each line says so.

  • Virallized (4.8 / 5, Top rated): A 7-day money-back guarantee on the first payment, no contract (its pricing page, 29 Oct 2026).
  • Sup Growth (4.7 / 5, Top rated): A 14-day free trial; a “money-back guarantee” is mentioned without published terms (25 Oct 2026).
  • Social Boost (4.0 / 5, Well rated): Its pricing page promises a full refund with “no fine print”; its refund policy page says refunds are case by case (23 Sept 2026).
  • Gainsty (3.5 / 5, Mixed reviews): A 7-day free trial; its refund policy mentions a 14-day money-back guarantee, makes yearly plans non-refundable and reviews other requests case by case.
  • Social Sensei (3.1 / 5, Mixed reviews): “14 Days Money Back Guarantee” on its plan cards; its FAQ adds that full refunds are not offered after that (3 Sept 2026).
  • BoostFluence (3.1 / 5, Mixed reviews): Pricing page (29 Oct 2026): "Satisfait ou remboursé 30 jours", a full refund within 30 days if you are not satisfied with the growth in the first month, after a 7-day free trial; cancel any time from the dashboard.
  • AiGrow (2.5 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Its homepage says 30-day money-back, its FAQ says 14-day; its Terms defer to the point of purchase (3 Sept 2026).
  • Trusy (1.8 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Its refund policy allows refunds only within 10 days of the first purchase and not once over half the allotted posts are serviced; Loops, Organic, Celebrity Partnerships and Brand Accelerator are non-refundable and the 10-day guarantee does not apply to the Elite package. Its sales page instead promises a full refund if unhappy "in the first 10 days of any month" (30 Oct 2026).
  • Path Social (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): 7-day money-back on a new purchase per its Terms; renewals not refundable (3 Sept 2026).
  • ProflUp (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): Full refund of a first payment on request within 30 days, no reason needed; each renewal refundable within 7 days; any undelivered part refundable at any time (return-policy page, 30 Oct 2026).

A refund only if the growth does not come

The promise is tied to results: money back if the account does not grow, judged by the service’s own terms.

  • GrowthSilo (3.3 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Refund policy (29 Oct 2026): a full refund of the first month if the account gains no followers matching the targeting brief in the first 30 days; after that, no refunds except where it failed to deliver or ended the service itself.
  • Outfame (2.5 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Its pricing cards say “Full refund if no net growth in 30 days”; its help centre says net growth “within the first 7 days” (18 Oct 2026).
  • UpGrow (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): A full monthly refund if there is no net-positive growth after the first 7 days, per its Terms (23 Sept 2026).
  • Plixi (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): “Guaranteed growth within 7 days or we'll give you a refund”, on its pricing page (23 Sept 2026).
  • TrendGram (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): Its Terms make yearly plans non-refundable, review performance refunds case by case with "no obligation", and give no refund once the 3-day trial has turned into a charge; the shortfall remedy is extending the billing month, while the site advertises "Guaranteed Results Or It's Free" (30 Oct 2026).
  • Crowd Ignite (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): Pricing page FAQ (29 Oct 2026): if you do not see follower growth within 7 days of subscribing you can report it and "they can get your money back"; cancel any time from billing settings.

Refunds case by case, or only for orders not delivered

No promise you can rely on: refunds at the company’s discretion, for non-delivery, or only what consumer law requires.

  • BuzzHike (4.6 / 5, Top rated): A 30-day refund “subject to review and approval”, at its discretion (3 Sept 2026).
  • My Social Following (4.2 / 5, Well rated): No returns once paid, but a refund is due if an order is considerably delayed or cannot be provided in full; once fully delivered the sale is final (policy page, 30 Oct 2026). Follower packages carry a "Lifetime Guarantee" of free replacement for any drop.
  • Poprey (3.4 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): A refund can be requested only if an order has not started within 48 hours of payment; delivered orders are non-refundable, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Twitter refunds are store credit only, and refunds take 3 to 25 business days (poprey.com/rules, 30 Oct 2026).
  • Viplikes (3.3 / 5, Mixed reviews): Refund policy page (29 Oct 2026): a full refund only if the order did not start in the stated time or was not delivered at all, a partial refund for any undelivered or dropped part, and no refund for changed minds, quality or unmet expectations.
  • Skweezer (3.3 / 5, Mixed reviews): No refunds once an order is completed and sent; refunds only for non-delivery claimed in writing within 7 days of ordering, and not when a delay was flagged before ordering (refund-policy page, 30 Oct 2026).
  • Thunderclap (3.2 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Refunds within 30 days only for orders that fail to deliver, a targeting error or a charge with no working order; nothing for engagement delivered as ordered, where a drop within 30 days gets a free refill; store credit is offered first (30 Oct 2026).
  • Upleap (2.9 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): A 7-day free trial; refunds full, partial or prorated at its discretion, per its Terms (23 Sept 2026).
  • Like4Like (2.6 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Refunds only where a limited money-back guarantee is explicitly stated, only for unused credits or services within that period, reviewed case by case; nothing already used is refunded (terms and conditions section 17, 30 Oct 2026).
  • Socialstud.io (2.3 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Cancel at any time; cancelling immediately refunds the unused days of the current period pro rata, while days already provided and completed periods are not refunded (refund-policy page, 30 Oct 2026).
  • Nitreo (2.2 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Refund requests reviewed case by case, per its Terms (23 Sept 2026).
  • Flock Social (2.1 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): A free trial; refunds case by case and yearly plans non-refundable, per its Terms (23 Sept 2026).
  • Socialmonk (1.6 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Subscriptions renew weekly or monthly until cancelled in writing; consumers get the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal with a full refund, and the terms warn of a 25 EUR fee for unjustified refund requests (terms of service, 30 Oct 2026).
  • GoreAd (Goread.io) (Suspended, Rating suspended by Trustpilot): Terms (29 Oct 2026): a full refund only on request within 24 hours of purchase and before the order starts, "no refunds once an order is delivered", and a 30-day refill for drops on the most recent order only.

No refunds, or no refund terms found

Payments are not refundable under its terms, or its site has no refund terms we could find.

  • Grow Your Social (4.5 / 5, Well rated): The 10-day free trial can be cancelled with no charge, but once a paid billing cycle is billed it is non-refundable, with no partial or prorated refunds, except where consumer law requires (terms and conditions section 8, 30 Oct 2026).
  • Kicksta (1.9 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Its growth promise extends the trial rather than refunding (23 Sept 2026).
  • Instazood (1.5 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): No terms or refund page found on its site (23 Sept 2026).

Before you pay

Read the terms, not just the pricing page: they are what applies. Then read the service’s one-star reviews for refunds that were refused. The services with a money-back guarantee, ranked.

Questions people ask

Which Instagram growth services give refunds?

10 of the 32 services here promise money back on the first payment: Virallized, Sup Growth, Social Boost, Gainsty, Social Sensei, BoostFluence, AiGrow, Trusy, Path Social and ProflUp. Each one’s terms are on this page.

Can I get a refund from an Instagram growth service?

It depends on its terms. Some promise money back within a set period, some only if growth does not come, and many only for orders not delivered or at their discretion.

Why do refund promises differ from the terms?

Pricing pages are written to sell; terms are written to apply. Several services here promise a refund on the pricing page and narrow it in their terms. The terms are what counts.
