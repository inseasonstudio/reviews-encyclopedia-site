How much do Instagram growth services cost? 2026 prices

By Sara Mihajlovic, analystUpdated 30 October 2026

The short answer

Managed growth services that publish a monthly price in US dollars start between $59 and $375 a month, with a median of $97 across 11 services. 8 of the services here price per package rather than per month; buying followers or engagement is against Meta’s spam policy.

What the price does not tell you

Two services at the same price can have very different records. Before you pay, read the one-star reviews and the refund terms: refund policies compared, and the ranking by Trustpilot record.

Questions people ask

How much does an Instagram growth service cost?

Managed services that publish a monthly price in US dollars start between $59 and $375 a month; the median starting price is $97. Follower packages are priced per order. Read on each service’s own site; the dates are in its case file.

What is the cheapest managed Instagram growth service?

Among the managed services here that publish a US dollar monthly price, the lowest starting price is $59 a month: TrendGram (rating suspended by trustpilot on Trustpilot) and Crowd Ignite (rating suspended by trustpilot on Trustpilot).

Is a cheaper growth service worse?

Not by price alone. Compare the Trustpilot record and the refund terms; the cheapest monthly plans here belong to services whose ratings are poor or suspended.
