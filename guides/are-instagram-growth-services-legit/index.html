Are Instagram growth services legit? What the 2026 record shows

By Sara Mihajlovic, analystUpdated 30 October 2026

The short answer

Some are, many are not. On 29 October 2026, 3 of the 73 services here were top rated on Trustpilot: Virallized, Sup Growth and BuzzHike. 19 were poorly rated, Trustpilot had withheld 7 ratings for breaching its guidelines, and it had removed 25 more growth sites entirely.

Three different things sold under one name

  • Managed growth: a team grows the account for you, monthly.
  • Follower packages: a number of followers, likes or views for a price. 35 services here sell these, some beside a subscription: BuzzHike, Upleap, Outfame, Nitreo, Flock Social, Instazood, UpGrow, Buzzoid, Famoid, Growthoid, iDigic, Like4Like, My Social Following, Poprey, ProflUp, RiseKarma, Rushmax, Skweezer, SocialFollow, Sns-Growth, Stormlikes, Thunderclap, Twicsy, UseViral, Views4You, Boostlikes, Blastup, Viplikes, Naizop, GetAFollower, Bulkoid, Media Mister, GoreAd (Goread.io), SocialWick and Viralyft.
  • Software tools: self-serve tools you run yourself.

Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow buying or selling engagement, so the second kind sells something Instagram’s owner prohibits.

What the reviews say most often

Across the poorly rated services here, the complaints that recur are followers reported as bots or fake, charges after cancelling, refunds refused and support that does not answer. Each case file lists its own, with counts and quotes.

What Instagram and Meta say

Recommendations on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram lists accounts that have repeatedly used misleading practices to build a following, such as buying likes, among those it may not recommend to people who do not follow them. Repeatedly engaged in misleading practices to build followings, such as purchasing ‘likes’.

Spam (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta's spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows. Attempting to or successfully selling, buying or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows, clicks and use of specific hashtags.

Inauthentic Behaviour (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta removes fake accounts involved in inauthentic behaviour, which is why follower counts built on fake accounts fall when a clean-out reaches them.

How to remove spam or bot followers on InstagramInstagram Help Centre

Instagram filters followers that are likely to be spam into a separate Potential spam list, which can be removed in one go without the accounts being told.

Questions people ask

Are Instagram growth services legit?

Some are, many are not. Of the 73 services in this encyclopedia, 3 are top rated on Trustpilot (Virallized, Sup Growth and BuzzHike), 19 are poorly rated, and Trustpilot has withheld 7 ratings for breaching its guidelines and removed 25 more profiles.

Is buying Instagram followers allowed?

No. Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows.

How can I tell if a growth service is fake?

Read its one-star reviews and check whether its Trustpilot rating is suspended, see whether its own site also sells follower packs, and watch your own Instagram insights after you start.
