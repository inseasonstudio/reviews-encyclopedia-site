Why none is recommended

Spam (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta's spam policy, which covers Instagram, does not allow selling, buying or exchanging engagement such as likes, views and follows. Attempting to or successfully selling, buying or exchanging for engagement, such as likes, shares, views, follows, clicks and use of specific hashtags.

Inauthentic Behaviour (Community Standards)Meta Transparency Centre

Meta removes fake accounts involved in inauthentic behaviour, which is why follower counts built on fake accounts fall when a clean-out reaches them.

Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials (14 August 2024)US Federal Trade Commission

The rule bans selling or buying fake indicators of social media influence, such as followers or views generated by a bot or hijacked account, where the buyer knew or should have known they were fake and used them to misrepresent their influence for a commercial purpose. It also bars a business from presenting a review site it controls as independent.

Questions people ask

What is the best site to buy Instagram followers?

None we can recommend. Buying followers breaks Meta’s spam policy, which covers Instagram. Of the 35 follower sellers with a case file here, 9 are poorly rated on Trustpilot, 3 have a rating Trustpilot suspended and 15 have had their profiles removed. The best service for real growth instead, by Trustpilot record, is Virallized: 4.8 out of 5 from 92 reviews, 2% one-star, read 9 November 2026. Next are Sup Growth (4.7 from 51) and Grow Your Social (4.6 from 134).

Do bought Instagram followers stay?

Not reliably. Meta removes fake accounts, so followers built on them can fall away when a clean-out reaches them.

Is it illegal to buy Instagram followers?

Meta’s rules do not allow it on Instagram. In the US, the FTC’s rule on fake reviews and testimonials also bans buying or selling fake indicators of social media influence, such as followers generated by a bot or hijacked account, in certain commercial cases.
