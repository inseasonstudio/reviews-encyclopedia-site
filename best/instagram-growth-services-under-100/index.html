The best Instagram growth services under $100 a month, ranked by Trustpilot

By Sara Mihajlovic, analystFigures read 9 November 2026

Short version

The best Instagram growth service under $100 a month, by Trustpilot record, is Virallized: 4.8 out of 5 from 92 reviews, 2% one-star, read 9 November 2026. Next is Grow Your Social (4.6 from 134). 11 more are poorly rated or have a rating Trustpilot suspended.

  • Listed: services whose lowest monthly plan is under $100. Prices in other currencies are converted at 1.436 Australian dollars and 0.896 euros to the US dollar (9 November 2026).
  • The order is Trustpilot’s score, then its number of reviews, read 9 November 2026. Virallized runs this site and is ranked by the same rule.

The ranking

  1. 1. Virallized

    Top rated

    4.8 out of 5 on Trustpilot · 92 reviews · 2% one-star · $99 to $299 a month

    From $99 a month. Virallized scores 4.8 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 92 reviews, 2% of them one-star. What reviewers praise most: fast, helpful support; followers who fit the account. Its three one- and two-star reviews report: followers lost after stopping; paid days on cancelling; follower quality and price.

  2. 2. Grow Your Social

    Top rated

    4.6 out of 5 on Trustpilot · 134 reviews · 10% one-star · A$109.99 to A$124.99 a month

    From $76.59 a month (about; its price is A$109.99). Grow Your Social scores 4.6 out of 5 on Trustpilot from 134 reviews, 10% of them one-star. What reviewers praise most: follower and engagement growth; responsive support and a named manager. The complaint that comes up most: unsolicited sales emails.
