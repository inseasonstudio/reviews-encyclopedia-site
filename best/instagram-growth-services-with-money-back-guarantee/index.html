Promises tied to results instead

These promise money back only if the growth does not come, judged by their own terms. Their refund terms, in their own words:

  • GrowthSilo (3.3 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Refund policy (29 Oct 2026): a full refund of the first month if the account gains no followers matching the targeting brief in the first 30 days; after that, no refunds except where it failed to deliver or ended the service itself.
  • Outfame (2.5 / 5, Poorly rated: avoid): Its pricing cards say “Full refund if no net growth in 30 days”; its help centre says net growth “within the first 7 days” (18 Oct 2026).

Questions people ask

Which Instagram growth services have a money-back guarantee?

By Trustpilot record on 29 October 2026: Virallized (4.8), Sup Growth (4.7), Social Boost (4.0), Gainsty (3.5), Social Sensei (3.1) and BoostFluence (3.1). Each one’s terms are on this page, in its own words.

Is a money-back guarantee enough to trust a growth service?

No. Several services promise one on their pricing page and narrow it in their terms. Read the terms, and the service’s one-star reviews, before you pay.

What is the difference between a money-back guarantee and a growth guarantee?

A money-back guarantee returns the first payment if you ask within a set period. A growth guarantee only pays out if the account does not grow, judged by the service’s own terms.
